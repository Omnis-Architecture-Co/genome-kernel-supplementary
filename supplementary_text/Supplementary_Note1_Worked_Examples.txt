SUPPLEMENTARY NOTE 1: ENCODING PIPELINE WORKED EXAMPLES
============================================================

Two complete walkthroughs trace biological sequences through the
6-bit encoding pipeline described in Methods. Each step shows
intermediate values to enable independent verification.

Worked Example: Insulin INS Locus (chr11) - DNA Pathway
============================================================

Genomic DNA sequence (first 51 nt of CDS):
  ATGGCCCTGTGGATGCGCCTCCTGCCCCTGCTGGCGCTGCTGGCCCTCTGG

Step 1: DNA to binary (A=00, T=01, G=10, C=11)
  ATGG -> 00011010
  CCCT -> 11111101
  GTGG -> 10011010
  ATGC -> 00011011
  GCCT -> 10111101
  CCTG -> 11110110
  ... (27 more nucleotides)

Step 2: Binary stream (first 48 bits):
  000110101111110110011010000110111011110111110110...

Step 3: Partition into 8-bit bytes
  Byte stream (first 12 of 12 bytes):
  Binary:  00011010  11111101  10011010  00011011  10111101  11110110
  Decimal: 26  253  154  27  189  246
  Hex:     1A  FD  9A  1B  BD  F6
           FF  6D  AE  DB  6B  F7

Note: The DNA pathway skips the amino-acid-to-codon step.
Binary encoding is applied directly to the genomic nucleotide
sequence, producing a byte stream that can be matched against
the same vocabulary used for protein-derived encodings.
